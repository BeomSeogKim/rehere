What SAM 3.1 (Meta Model API) outlines in the lodge photographs: model estimates, not measurements

Three public photographs of the Grand Canyon Lodge, each asked about ten everyday nouns, one noun per request. 127 outlines came back in 30 requests.

Back to the three photographs

How to read this page

  • These are 2D outlines proposed by SAM 3.1 (Meta Model API) for each photograph. They are estimates, not measurements, and there is no ground truth for these photographs here.
  • Confidence is the number the model returned with each outline. It is not a measure of accuracy.
  • Missed objects and merged or loose outlines are left in. A noun for which the model returned nothing shows zero, and that is an observed answer, not proof that nothing is there.
  • Nothing here is a 3D model, a stitched room or a reconstruction. These are fresh requests made for this page; they are not the outlines used in the reconstructed scene of the entry.
  • 30 requests, one image each, list price about USD 0.075 (an estimate from the published price, not a bill). Masks were decoded offline with the official meta-sam-parser==0.1.0 parser; each overlay image has exactly the pixel size of its photograph.
