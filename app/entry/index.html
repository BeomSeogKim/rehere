New York's original Penn Station, demolished in 1963

Explore New York's original Penn Station, demolished in 1963, through an archival photograph. This partial scene shows only visible surfaces. A small change of viewpoint reveals empty and torn edges where the photograph cannot show more.

Photograph: Cervin Robinson, 1962. HABS NY-5471. Source and file title: Wikimedia Commons source record. Rights: U.S. Government work. No known restrictions on images made by the U.S. Government.

Original and approved versions

The primary link opens Saved version 1, the original saved arrangement. Replacement audio is labelled Synthetic voice (OpenAI TTS), not a witness or a person's recollection. Its supplied synthesis script is the locked 185-byte text. One 0-9950 ms whole-track cue is copied manually from that script, not from ASR or sentence alignment; it does not verify the heard words. Listening and editorial review are pending. This static editorial replacement is not a newly saved story in the running app. Historical Muse Voice Transcribe captions belong to the earlier audio and were not reused as processing provenance for this replacement.

See Saved version 2: the approved clock turn

Saved version 2 records a clock turn of -10 degrees with zero translation. The clock change is a demonstration hypothesis, not a witness's memory. It is a Muse Spark proposal previewed and approved by the demo owner. Saved version 1 remains available; the synthetic story is attached only to Saved version 1.

Scene outlines: local SAM 2.1, with local depth estimation. This scene does not use SAM 3.1. The photograph, synthetic notes and voice, and model estimates are shown as what they are; none establishes verified history.

What this demo is, and is not

  • The scene is partial, with empty and torn edges and a tiny viewing range of at most 10 degrees from the photograph's viewpoint, rather than a complete reconstructed room. Use the photograph comparison to inspect its limits.
  • Story, voice and notes are synthetic demonstration material, not personal memories or witness accounts. Saved version 2 is an approved demonstration hypothesis; the original saved version is not overwritten.
  • This is a frozen, read-only offline copy. Withdrawal is enforced in the running app; this copy does not follow later withdrawal. Copies remain until removed from the file server, and copies already saved on a device cannot be removed from here.
